How to install the APK on Android

Because StepPulse is free and open-source, you install the APK directly on your phone in 4 simple steps.

1

Download the APK

Click the "Download APK" button on this page using your Android phone’s browser (Chrome, Firefox, etc.).

2

Confirm Download

If your browser shows "File might be harmful", tap Download anyway (standard Android warning for direct APKs).

3

Allow Unknown Sources

Tap the downloaded notification. If prompted, toggle on "Allow from this source" for your browser.

4

Tap Install & Grant Sensor

Tap Install. Once opened, tap Allow for Physical Activity permission, and you're all set!
