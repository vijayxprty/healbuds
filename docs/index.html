Everything Built For You, 100% On-Device

No third-party trackers, no mandatory sign-ups, no monthly fees. Your health data stays in your phone's SQLite database.

👟

Hardware Pedometer & 10-Step Cadence

Uses Android TYPE_STEP_COUNTER hardware coprocessor. Features a 10-step buffer to ignore pocket twitches and vehicle vibration rejection. Persists through reboots.

🍱

Authentic Indian Thali Diet Planner

Visual interactive Thali plate with Rajma, Curd, Rice, Salad, Dal, and Roti. Veg/Non-Veg toggle, custom dish builder with food emojis, and personalized calorie targets.

🔥

Smart Streaks & 2 Rest Days/Week

Recovery is health. You get 2 guilt-free rest days per week (Mon to Sun) without breaking your streak. Only resets if inactive 3 days in a row.
